# LAST IMPACT R07.8.4 — Rival deployment

The two humanoid rivals use the original RAZE models with separate blue/amber armor tints and the original articulated glider GLB. Four drone rivals remain at their existing ground spawn points. No additional humanoid meshes were generated. The actual actors are reused, not duplicated on landing; cosmetic seated cabin passengers remain separate from the rival bodies.

The rival departure sequence starts once the player leaves the aircraft, staggered at 0.25 and 0.90 seconds. Each rival steers toward a different open zone (-22,0,-24 and 22,0,24), uses the established flight controller, opens its wings automatically and sweeps the physical capsule against world geometry. Airborne rivals have collision layer 0, are not damageable by ground fire or storm, and do not run ground AI until both they and the player have landed. The drone roster and training scene are unchanged. The map distinguishes airborne rivals with blue rings. The player blends from the last cabin run pose instead of resetting immediately on exit. Particles for rivals are range-gated at 18m and use the low profile. This is still an offline prototype, not a multiplayer server.

Local headless Godot 4.6 tests: manual staggered launch/landing/reset, automatic launch/interrupted reset/second round, R0783 audio/cabin/flight fixture, smoke, touch and clip gameplay. A known dummy renderer material-null message at scene destruction remains visible in logs. Visual/mobile performance and physical audio are not acceptance-tested; no APK generated.
