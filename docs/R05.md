# R05 Animation prototype
Preserves R04 source and optimized GLB. Uses 14 named joints, cached rest-space axes, smoothed procedural poses driven by actual velocity, breathing, walk, sprint, jump/fall/land, slide and aim pose. Hand sockets are groundwork for R06; no weapon yet. Preview camera closer, original dance toggle. Android conservative renderer unchanged. Not authored mocap or foot IK; verify clipping, foot sliding and pose direction on physical device before promoting. Tests cover state transitions and bone rotation, not visual quality.
