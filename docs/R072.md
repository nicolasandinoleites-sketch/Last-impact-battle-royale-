# R07.2 — Mixed opponent roster

Based on R07.1 ead41671aaa6e2e57c723601ae6e0de76eee2f0b. Survival roster: four drones, two humanoid opponents with independent RAZE skeleton instances, tinted materials and markers. Original imported assets retained. Operators use capsule collision, gravity, actual horizontal displacement for procedural motion, basic pursuit and short obstacle detours. Arcade contact damage only, no human combat simulation or advanced navigation. Animations update at 20 Hz near the player and 10 Hz farther away; distant opponents skip full leg IK. This is a CPU optimization hypothesis, not measured device performance. Fallback capsule if character import fails. Round reset restores opponent health, collision and pose; player physics pauses after victory/defeat and resumes on restart.

Smoke assertions cover independent rigs, mixed roster, movement, animation update, reset and victory. Visual captures 22 and 23 inspect the operator. No executed validation confirmed. APK withheld. Full humanoid art, navigation mesh, expanded map and online multiplayer still pending.
