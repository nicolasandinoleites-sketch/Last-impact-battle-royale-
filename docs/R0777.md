# R07.7.7 — FRONTIER landing foundation

Based on R07.7.6 cabin cinematic (29f393f). The flight controller now accepts an optional surface sampler. It samples under the current feet for automatic glider deployment and under the predicted horizontal destination before touchdown. Existing constant-height callers remain supported. The new adapter raycasts world collision layer 1 and exposes a standing-capsule clearance check using the real player dimensions. The fixture uses physical static floor and roof colliders with FRONTIER dimensions; it does not load the entire map.

The existing gameplay entry point is deliberately unchanged until the articulated GLB assets and full cabin sequence can be recovered and integrated. Side-wall collision, camera handoff, capsule clearance enforcement, HUD, AI and zone pausing, audio, and Android physical QA are still pending. No APK published.
