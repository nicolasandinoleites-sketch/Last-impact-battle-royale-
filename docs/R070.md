# R07 — Offline survival prototype

Based on R06.3 third review, with original character and fictional IGNIS assets preserved. Training remains a separate scene. Survival features six lightweight arcade drones, a circular zone with 18-second grace and contraction from 34 to 6 world units over 132 seconds, contact damage and zone damage, health and shield accounting, victory/defeat, reset, minimap circle, timer, and lobby mode selection. Drones use simple pursuit and a short obstacle detour, not navmesh or human opponents. No bot-versus-bot combat or multiplayer yet. Drone meshes are temporary procedural assets, not new GLB characters. No loot or inventory yet.

Smoke tests cover mode startup, drone movement, contraction, outside-zone damage, shield, defeat, restart and victory. Visual test captures 17–19 show startup, contracted zone, and result. These are tests added in source, not confirmed execution.

Release policy: validation workflow should run on this branch; do not distribute an APK until successful automated checks, visual review and device verification. Android performance and animation remain unverified.
