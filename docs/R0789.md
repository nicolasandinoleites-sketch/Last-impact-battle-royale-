# R07.8.9 — Arsenal and map loot
Four fictional gameplay profiles with different damage, range, cadence, magazine, reload, and ammo family. Original Tripo IGNIS rig shared as temporary color variants (not unique meshes). Three weapon/ammo pairs are seeded into distinct walkable sectors. Acquired weapons retain magazine and family reserves when switched with TAB or ARMA touch button. Standard survival/training retain IGNIS and original nine pickups. Rival operators retain the previous IGNIS combat balance. Classes, new original geometry, rival loadouts and vehicles are not yet implemented.
