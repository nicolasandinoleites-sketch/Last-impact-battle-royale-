# R07.3 — FRONTIER

Base R07.2, preserved as an independent branch. Seven stages in source:

1. Survival arena expands from 80x80 to 128x128 world units; original training unchanged. Initial zone radius 54; grace 25 seconds, contraction 180 seconds to radius 6.
2. Four procedural 10x10 buildings with two open entrances, walls, roof and interior floor. No stairs or roof access yet.
3. Four visually distinct surface sectors HELIX, NEXUS, FORGE, HARBOR; shared cached textures, flat terrain (no elevation).
4. Shared 64x64 AStarGrid2D, obstacle masks, periodically refreshed paths for four drones and two operators; original short detour fallback retained. Basic arcade AI only.
5. Three fixed starter supplies and six seeded unique positions per round; quantity/capacity rules unchanged.
6. Minimap scale 128 units, sector fills, footprints, north, current sector, circle and markers.
7. Three instanced decoration batches, cached mipmapped textures and optional trim on medium/high profiles. Smoke checks for map, route and loot; visual captures 24 and 25. CPU/GPU/device performance unmeasured.

Validation gate: PR and push workflow, no automatic APK export. Test and visual results must be inspected, followed by real Android trial. The source is a prototype, not a verified release; no multiplayer, terrain elevation or unique enemy mesh yet.

## QA review 1
CI run 38 completed asset import and initial smoke checks, then failed the original nominal stance-speed assertion. The old cadence forced a 0.36 m half-step clamp even at 1.5 m/s. Revised cadence derives from stance duty and the reach budget; tests now cover three reachable speeds and separate the capped sprint case. Removed unsupported SVG boot splash reference, retaining the icon and lobby. High-speed foot slip remains a known limitation pending visual inspection. No APK released.

## QA review 2
Run 43 passed import, full smoke, desktop visuals and safe material capture; 58 desktop frames and 2 safe frames generated. Manual contact sheet review confirms visible imported player and opponent, open interiors, and pickup graphics. Identified survival HUD buttons near legs and overly plain facades. Moved healing/shield/pickup controls, improved button symbols and message contrast; added non-colliding facade trim and ceiling accents in existing three batches. Added safe-profile survival capture. These follow-up changes require a fresh CI pass. Desktop safe render is not an Android device test; no APK released.
