# R0792 — Operator classes
Five player classes, persisted selection in lobby. RAZE reduces shot interval, PHANTOM reduces AI humanoid detection and increases movement (not invisibility), TITAN halves combat damage (not storm damage; no physical barrier yet), SERAPH restores personal HP and shield, SPECTRE reveals nearby enemies on minimap. Ground combat only; cooldown includes active duration and resets each match. UI button and Q. Original RAZE model shared temporarily. No separate Tripo models, team abilities or AI ability activation. Automated regression fixture; no Android device testing or APK.
