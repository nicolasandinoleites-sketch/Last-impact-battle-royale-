# R07.9.0 — Two weapon slots and battle supplies
Based on r0789-arsenal-loot (CI approved). Battle Royale only; training and legacy survival unchanged.
- Maximum two firearm types carried. Picking up a third exchanges the active one; discarded weapon retains loaded ammunition and purchased attachment metadata. Ammo banks stay in inventory.
- Separate energy blade and pulse grenade controls (V/B or touch); ray-checked melee and fictional area effect, capped at four carried grenades. Primitive prototype meshes; final animation and models pending.
- Store grants 300 match credits; weapon costs 200 and includes quick reload. Extended magazine (100 credits, +25% rounded up) and reload module (125 credits, -20% duration) per firearm. Purchases only within 3 m. One displayed available weapon at a time; no persistent gunsmith or 3D accessory meshes yet.
- Four seeded map pickups (credits/grenades) in four sectors, total 19 static pickups. One automatic airdrop per round after 35 seconds of ground gameplay; 4-second descent, 250 credits, 2 grenades, HELIOS. Indicator on minimap. All dynamic drops and upgrades clear on reset.
- Eleven local regression fixtures passed on Godot 4.6; screenshots rendered using OpenGL Compatibility. No device benchmarks or APK. No additional tools required. AI does not yet use the new equipment or shop.
